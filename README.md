An **AI-powered face recognition platform** designed for professional photography agencies covering weddings and events.

Guests can identify themselves and automatically discover the photographs in which they appear. Instead of manually browsing through hundreds or thousands of wedding photos, the platform uses **AI facial recognition** to find matching images and make the guest's complete personal photo collection available for viewing and download.

**Highlights:** AI Face Recognition • Wedding Photography • Image Matching • Photo Discovery • Guest Experience • Automation
